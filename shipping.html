Shipping

How a gift travels

We pack to order from Thenkulapakkam, Tamil Nadu, usually within four to seven working days after PayU confirms the till. Courier time then depends on the PIN.

A flat shipping charge is added at checkout unless the goods already reach the free-shipping threshold shown on the till. We deliver across India to a valid 6-digit PIN. Tracking is written on your account page when the parcel leaves.

Bulk gifts of twenty-five or more are not shipped through this checkout; we plan those with you.
