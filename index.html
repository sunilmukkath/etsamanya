The pantry principle

Five things we will not compromise.

Samanya means the everyday. We treat the kitchen as a place of healing — spice, nut, fruit of the tree — packed so someone feels tended.

01Natural

Whole spices, unpolished nuts, nothing dyed to look louder than the harvest.

02Traditional

Recipes and pairings a grandmother would recognise: a thaal, a masala dabba, a festival box.

03Indian

From Kerala vines and Kashmir orchards — gifts that taste of this land, not a warehouse aisle.

04Locally sourced

We work with growers and small millers, not anonymous bulk lots with a pretty ribbon.

05Freshest

Spices and dry fruits packed to order from the current lot. A gift should still smell alive.
