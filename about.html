The name

We do not gift objects. We gift what will be cooked, served, and spoken.

samanya is a wellness gifting house. We curate boxes and potlis of spices, dry fruits, and serveware — then a mindful line — so the person who opens them feels tended.

Healing, here, is not a claim. It is a pepper still bright from the mill. Almonds that have not sat in a painted tin. A bowl they will eat from. A sentence they will read twice.

Personalisation is asking who needs nourishment: what they cook, what they cannot eat, what words would rest them. Then packing a gift that could only have been meant for them.

Natural. Traditional. Indian. Locally sourced. The everyday kitchen, given as care. That is the whole manifesto.

How we source

From vine, orchard, and mill — not from a catalogue.

Spice

Known origins

Malabar pepper, Byadgi and Kashmiri chilli, Erode turmeric, green cardamom — named for the land, not a blend number.

Orchard

Current lots

Mamra almonds, cashews, pistachios, anjeer, raisins. We pack what is bright this week, and we say so.

Craft

Small hands

Growers, millers, and packers we can telephone. If we cannot tell you where it came from, it does not enter the box.

If someone needs tending, we have a pantry.
